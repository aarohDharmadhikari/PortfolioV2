## `01` — The Space

A personal portfolio documenting my work, interests, and evolution as a developer.

Built around a simple idea: a portfolio should be more than a list of technologies. It should reflect the person behind the code, the problems they choose to solve, and the ideas they bring to life.

This is the second iteration of that idea — a renewed approach to design, structure, and presentation, shaped by lessons from the previous version.
